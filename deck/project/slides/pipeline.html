Layer one learns what real calls look like and where they fail. Layer two tries a prompt change cheaply: Sarvam rewrites only the section the change touches, and two Sarvam agents hold the conversation, the VANI bot on prompt A or B and a buyer modelled on a real recorded caller. Like live VANI, the bot is given the product's quantity units and spec fields, here from IndiaMART's category data, so it asks what a real call asks; a blind judge scores both. Layer three is the A/B engine the brief asks for: sticky hash routing, a group-sequential test with five planned looks, automatic promote or rollback, a named approver when the evidence conflicts, and an append-only decision log.
