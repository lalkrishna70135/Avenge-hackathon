The problem

One prompt edit changes thousands of buyer calls at once

Changes ship blind

A rewrite of VANI's 163 KB prompt goes straight to every buyer, with no evidence that it helps.

Effects are small

A 3-point change in leads hides in noise. Peeking at results daily inflates false wins.

No safe rollback

A harmful change keeps losing leads until someone notices. Nothing records why it shipped.

Three problems: prompt changes ship with no evidence, the effects that matter are small enough to hide in noise, and when a change hurts there is no automatic way back and no record of the decision. The brief asks for config, routing, simulation, statistics, decisions, rollout and a log.
