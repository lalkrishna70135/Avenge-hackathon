Limits and next steps

What we would do with one more week

Honest limits today

  • Simulated buyers are models seeded from real calls; at 60 per change, lead effects sit inside the noise
  • Duration is a minute-resolution timestamp proxy, so the guardrail margin is 15%
  • Audit verdicts come from a model, not human QA
  • The simulated bot runs a condensed prompt, with spec fields from a category match

Next

  • Calibrate simulated buyers against real outcomes, then scale the pilot
  • Use real talk time from call records for the duration guardrail
  • Check the audit against human QA labels on a sample
  • Test more than two variants at once (A/B/n)
We are explicit about what is not finished. The simulated pilot ran in full: sixty personas per change plus an A/A check, which showed that with sixty buyers, lead effects are within noise, so simulation is for behaviour and the live A/B test is for leads. The statistical problem we found ourselves, repeat callers pushing false harm-stops to 7.1 percent, is fixed by scoring each buyer on a fixed window from their first call: now 5.0 percent. The simulated bot runs a condensed version of the 163 KB prompt with the changed sections verbatim, and gets spec fields by matching the product to IndiaMART's category data; 10 of 60 products had no match. Everything in the brief, config, routing, simulation, sequential statistics, decisions, rollout, approvals and the log, is implemented and tested: 66 automated tests pass.
